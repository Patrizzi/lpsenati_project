# Surface Brief: TechStock Operational Suite

- **Mode:** operate
- **Scope:** Full 9-screen inventory management application (`login`, `dashboard`, `products`, `categories`, `suppliers`, `movements`, `alerts`, `reports`, `profile`)
- **Audience & Task:** System administrator controlling technology inventory, auditing SKU stock against minimum thresholds, recording atomic stock movements, and importing/exporting CSV data.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** TechStock operates as a high-precision Swiss editorial plate register on uncoated stock, refusing the generic dark-slate-and-neon SaaS dashboard rut in favor of crisp 1px hairlines, corner registration crosshairs, and absolute typographic clarity.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Uncoated near-white stock (`#F5F4F6` drifting subtly between warm `#FAF6F5` and cool `#EEF3EF`), deep carbon ink (`#16181A`), exposed `1px` hairlines (`#C9C7C4`), periwinkle verification seals (`#DCDDF3`), semantic alert ink (`#B91C1C` critical, `#B45309` threshold), sharp `0px` radius rectangular buttons (`#16181A` fill with `11px` tracked `0.14em` mono caps), and a rotated circular SVG `textPath` verification stamp (`25s linear infinite`).
- **STORY:** The administrator perceives every inventory figure, SKU, and stock movement as an audited, tamper-proof record where low-stock deficits (`stock <= stock_minimo`) stand out immediately without visual noise.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** A `64px` sticky top token bar (`11px` mono uppercase, `0.14em` tracking) and a `240px` left module index rail frame the working sheet; the header pairs a `52px` light-weight grotesque title (`400`, `-0.02em`) with a rotating periwinkle SVG verification seal (`#DCDDF3`) and a sharp `#16181A` primary CTA (`+ Nuevo producto` / `Registrar movimiento`), above a 4-column hairline metric strip registered with `+` corner crosshairs and a two-column split of active low-stock alerts and atomic movement history.
- **FORM:** Challenger `design-annual-s-plate-section` (Pliego de Registro Editorial, challenger #3 from the roll); `seed:c4468a4cbfca85b4`.
